What We Do

Your spine health specialists

A full set of in-office therapies, matched to what your body needs to move and feel better.

Chiropractic Adjustment

Targeted adjustment and manipulation to restore movement and ease back, neck and joint pain.

Cold Laser Therapy

Gentle low-level laser to support tissue and help calm inflammation as part of your care plan.

Medical Acupuncture

Acupuncture from a Dipl.Ac. provider, used to address pain and support recovery alongside chiropractic care.

Physical Therapy

Therapeutic exercise, ultrasound, and electrical and interferential muscle stimulation to rebuild strength.

Intersegmental Traction

Traction-table therapy to gently mobilize the spine and support the discs between adjustments.

Digital X-Rays

On-site digital imaging so we can see what is going on and build the right plan from your first visit.

Auto & Work Injury Care

Hurt in a crash or on the job? Get evaluated.

After an accident, pain from whiplash and soft-tissue injuries can take days to show up. The sooner you are evaluated, the better we can care for you and the cleaner your records are if you end up needing them.

  • Evaluation for whiplash, sciatica and disc injuries
  • Care plans for sports, auto and work injuries
  • Clear records kept from your first visit
  • Two locations to fit your week

What patients ask us

Which conditions does Chiro Health Plus treat?+
We care for back, neck and shoulder pain, sciatica, whiplash, carpal tunnel, disc injuries, knee pain, and sports, auto and work injuries. Treatment may include chiropractic adjustment, cold laser therapy, medical acupuncture and physical therapy. Call (513) 251-2273 to be seen.
Do you treat auto and work injuries?+
Yes. Auto and work injury care is a core focus here. We evaluate your injury, build a care plan, and keep clear records from your first visit.
Which location should I visit?+
We have two Cincinnati offices. Western Hills is at 5948 Glenway Ave, open Monday, Wednesday and Friday. Reading is at 8030 Reading Rd, Suite 6, open Tuesday and Thursday. Pick whichever is closer and we will get you scheduled.
